What the numbers mean

Tier 0–6 is Fretwork's difficulty score mapped onto the same spread as official in-game tiers, per instrument. Open-ended tier keeps going past 6 for very hard charts. D is the raw score behind both.

Movement is average hand movement per second (fret changes, or newly struck pads on drums). Spread is how much the note rate swings over the song: low for an even chart, high for one with spikes. Drums also show kicks (single-pedal kicks per second) and, when the chart has double kick, the open-ended tier counting it (2x).

Only Expert is rated. Fretwork rates 5-fret guitar, bass, rhythm, co-op, keys and drums; 6-fret and vocals aren't rated.

Ratings use Fretwork by Staycation (MIT licence, formulas v3), ported to Rust. Chart listings and files come from Chorus Encore; each song links to its page there. Not affiliated with either. Charts belong to their charters.
