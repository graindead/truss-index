What the numbers mean

Fretwork works out how hard a chart is from its notes, not from the difficulty the charter typed in. Only Expert is rated: 5-fret guitar, bass, rhythm, co-op, keys and drums (not 6-fret or vocals).

What it measures

A one-second window slides through the chart a quarter-second at a time, counting:

Notes
Notes per second. A chord counts as one note, as in the game.
Movement
How much your hand changes per second: the frets lifted or added between one note and the next. Green → Yellow is 1, Green → Green-Yellow-Blue is 2. A fast passage on one fret has lots of notes but little movement.
Drums
Hands (pad hits per second), travel (newly struck pads, like movement) and kicks per second. Charted roll lanes count at a capped rate.

D, the Fretwork score

For each count, Fretwork blends three numbers from the song: its busiest second, its average and its typical (median) second. So one hard burst helps, but not as much as being hard all the way through. The results are added together, then adjusted twice:

  • Unevenness: songs that swing between bursts and rests get a small boost.
  • Length: a song of about 3 minutes 50 seconds counts as normal. A 1-minute chart scores about 25% lower, a 6-minute one about 10% higher.

Higher D means harder. Each instrument has its own scale, so compare guitar with guitar and drums with drums.

The other numbers

Tier 0–6
D sorted into seven bands per instrument, set so official charts spread across them roughly like the in-game difficulty dots. Stops at 6.
Open-ended tier
Keeps counting past 6. Each tier is roughly 15–20% more D than the one before, so very hard charts can reach 10 or more.
Harder than
The share of rated charts on this site, on the same instrument, with a lower D.
Movement
Average movement per second over the whole song.
Spread
How much the note rate swings over the song. Lower is steadier; higher means bursts and breaks.
Kicks, 2x
Average single-pedal kicks per second. If the chart has double kick, the tier counting those notes too is shown as well.
Graph
Click an instrument in a chart's details. The D line at each moment is notes plus movement (or hands, travel and kicks), smoothed over 2 seconds, without the length and unevenness adjustments.

Full details are in Fretwork's methodology.
